# LAWYER LAILY — Project scope

Two products: (1) Bangladesh legal research AI agent, (2) AI-powered mobile application.

## Trusted source network
- Official legislation and Constitution: https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/
- Legislative Division: https://legislativediv.gov.bd/
- Gazette archive: https://www.dpp.gov.bd/
- Supreme Court: https://www.supremecourt.gov.bd/
- Judiciary court directory: https://judiciary.gov.bd/
- Subordinate court cause lists: https://causelist.judiciary.gov.bd/
- Bangladesh Bank: https://www.bb.org.bd/
- Bangladesh Financial Intelligence Unit: https://www.bfiu.org.bd/
- University Grants Commission: https://ugc.gov.bd/
- Parliamentary Library: https://catalog.parliament.gov.bd/
- Dhaka University Law Journal: https://journals.du.ac.bd/index.php/dulj
- Rajshahi University Law Journal: https://csg.ru.ac.bd/lawjournal/

## Safety and reliability
Do not invent case citations, court dates or statutory sections. Track legal status, amendment dates, provenance, retrieval date, licensing and permitted access for each source. Treat commentary separately from binding law. Never claim complete nationwide coverage without confirmation.

## Milestones
1. Build constitutional article registry and verified source links.
2. Searchable law, gazette, policy and university library sources.
3. RAG answers with official citations.
4. Court lists and case dashboard where public access is available.
5. Mobile client and authenticated lawyer dashboard.
