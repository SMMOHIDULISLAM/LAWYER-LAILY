# LAWYER LAILY — Bangladesh Legal Source Network

## Product scope
1. AI Agent: retrieval, legal research, citation validation, source change detection.
2. AI-powered mobile app: bilingual chat, statute browse, cause-list navigation, user-authorized case dashboard.

## Official primary sources
- Legislation, Constitution, historical instruments: https://bdlaws.minlaw.gov.bd/
- Legislative and Parliamentary Affairs Division: https://legislativediv.gov.bd/
- Bangladesh gazettes: https://www.dpp.gov.bd/
- Supreme Court judgments and case services: https://www.supremecourt.gov.bd/
- Judiciary portal: https://judiciary.gov.bd/
- District and subordinate court cause lists: https://causelist.judiciary.gov.bd/
- Bangladesh Bank and circular library: https://www.bb.org.bd/
- Bangladesh Financial Intelligence Unit: https://www.bfiu.org.bd/
- National Board of Revenue: https://nbr.gov.bd/
- BSEC regulations: https://sec.gov.bd/
- UGC Bangladesh university master directory: https://ugc.gov.bd/
- Parliament Library catalogue: https://catalog.parliament.gov.bd/

## Secondary research discovery links
- Dhaka University Law Journal: https://journals.du.ac.bd/index.php/dulj
- Rajshahi University law journal: https://csg.ru.ac.bd/lawjournal/
- BRAC University law subject guide: https://library.bracu.ac.bd/subject-guides/law
- Supreme Court Bar Association: https://scba.org.bd/

These are entry points rather than a complete university-by-university library audit. Verify each catalog, journal, current URL, license, and permissible indexing workflow individually.

## Source priority and verification
1. Official promulgated text, commencement notices, and official gazette.
2. Applicable authoritative judicial decisions.
3. Official regulatory circulars and directives (with issue and effective dates).
4. Academic literature and commercial legal commentary as *secondary* material.

Every document or section must include its official source link, language, instrument identifier, date of legal effect (if known), repeal/supersession relationships, source retrieval date and verification state. Never treat unknown repeal status as proof that a law is currently operative. Never invent judicial citations, case statuses or hearing dates.

## Access constraints
Do not bypass logins, captchas or rate limits. Bar and university library catalogues do not confer a right to copy copyrighted books. Public case listings may still contain sensitive personal information; limit retention and restrict access to saved-case dashboards.

## Milestones
- M1: Official Constitution article-by-article database, version history and cited commentary.
- M2: All-law title catalogue with exact identifiers and deduplication.
- M3: Supreme Court and district court source registry and manual cause-list portal links.
- M4: Bangladesh Bank circular tracking and supersession graph.
- M5: UGC directory audit, institutional law journals, book metadata catalogue.
- M6: Hybrid search + citation-grounded RAG + human-reviewed quality evaluation.
- M7: Shared API for mobile app; privacy, authentication, audit logs and notifications.
